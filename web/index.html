How the model earns your trust

Tested on leaves it never saw

Rotated and flipped copies of the same leaf were kept together, so no leaf in the test set had a twin in training. The accuracy you see is honest.

Ready for real fields

Besides clean lab photos, it learned from messy outdoor photos with soil, sky and other plants in the background, and was tested on them too.

Shows its reasoning

A Grad-CAM heat-map lights up the patches of the leaf that drove each answer, so you can see whether it looked at the lesions or at the background.
